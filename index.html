🎯 Decision Engine — Modelled Probability of FDA Approval

Transparent Bayesian odds calculator. Starts from published base rate (FDA / BIO-Informa), applies documented odds multipliers (review pathway, company track record, asset characteristics, advisory committee), and prints arithmetic so any reviewer can redo it. Not investment advice — decision-support only.

Scientific Basis: Base rates from BIO / Biomedtracker / Informa Pharma Intelligence, Clinical Development Success Rates 2011-2020 (NDA/BLA → approval 90.6%, n=1,453; Phase III → NDA/BLA 57.8%; Phase I → approval 7.9%). Data-quality gate (v19, 2026-09-19). The engine's project-data inputs are only as complete as the year they come from: data/decision_engine_year_inputs.csv scores every year 1977–2026 against FDA's official NME counts (17 MATCH years at 1.0; short years must be down-weighted, never treated as the full approval population). 📐 The Scoring Model (0-100)

Total score (0–100) = Outcome (30) + Market validation (25) + Experience (20) + Pathway quality (15) + Pipeline progression (10) Outcome (30 pts) = 30 × clamp( (shrunk_rate − 0.75) / 0.25, 0, 1 ) where shrunk_rate = (approvals + k·p₀) / (decisions + k), k = 3, p₀ = 0.906 p₀ is VERIFIED population base rate for filed NDA/BLA (BIO / Biomedtracker / Informa, 2011–2020, n = 1,453) Market validation (25 pts) = 25 × [ 0.6 × share of priced approval events with positive T+1 move + 0.4 × clamp(median T+1 move / +25%, 0, 1) ] Experience (20 pts) = 20 × clamp( log2(1 + decisions tracked) / log2(1 + 12), 0, 1 ) Pathway quality (15 pts) = 15 × [ share Priority Review − 0.5 × share Accelerated Approval ] (clamped 0…1) Pipeline progression (10) = 10 × advanced_to_next_phase / (advanced + paused/clinical-hold) — only where pipeline scorecard is verified deep dive; otherwise 10 pts redistributed pro-rata and company labelled lower confidence.
  • Shrinkage toward verified external base rate, not zero. Outcome used to be Wilson 95% lower bound. That punished thin evidence asymmetrically: 1 approval/0 CRLs scored ~21%, implying 79% failure rate that no published dataset supports — FDA approves ~90.6% of filed applications. Now shrinks toward verified prior with k=3 pseudo-observations, so one approval scores 92.9% and company only departs from base rate once enough verified decisions justify it.
  • Calibration band keeps component discriminating. Because shrinkage compresses toward 90.6%, shrunk rate mapped through 75%–100% band anchored on published spread (BIO per-disease-area filing-to-approval 82.5% cardiovascular to 100% allergy). Population base rate earns 0.62 of 30 points — average filer, not top score — while sponsor with CRL falls sharply. Raw rate and Wilson lower bound still published as statistics.
  • Market validation and Experience re-weighted upward (20→25 and 15→20): market term only component built on exchange data, longer verified record main protection against reading luck as skill.
  • Accelerated approval treated as risk factor (−0.5 weight) because granted on surrogate endpoint that confirmatory trial must later verify; several accelerated approvals in dataset later withdrawn (Ukoniq, Relyvrio, Pepaxto, Exkivity).
  • Confidence reported alongside every score: High = ≥8 verified decisions and deep-dive pipeline; Moderate = ≥3 decisions; Low = fewer, or FDA-decisions-only view. Scores never presented without confidence tier.
  • Grades: A ≥80, B 65–79, C 50–64, D 35–49, E <35.

🔍 Verification Audit — Every Master Row Re-checked Against Its Primary Source

A source link is only a claim that a row matches its source. This audit actually opens the primary openFDA Drugs@FDA record for each of the 1,427 novel-approval rows and compares the sponsor, brand name and decision date field by field, so a wrong date or a swapped company is caught mechanically rather than by eye.

What the statuses mean. MATCH — date, brand and sponsor all agree with openFDA. MATCH_DATE_ONLY — date and brand agree, sponsor wording differs (ownership change or legal-entity naming). MATCH_VIA_GENERIC — openFDA publishes current labelling, so a discontinued or genericised product shows its generic/successor name; the generic reconciles. DATE_DIFFERS_FROM_DRUGSFDA_1_3D — FDA's published NME approval report and Drugs@FDA differ by 1–3 days on the same approval; both sources are official, this is a known reporting difference, not an error. NO_APPL_NUMBER — the row cites no Drugs@FDA application number, so it cannot be machine-cross-checked here (it still carries its FDA source links). NOT_IN_OPENFDA — the application is cited but openFDA returns no ORIG/AP record (common for legacy BLAs and transferred applications). MISMATCH_DATE / MISMATCH_BRAND — genuine source-vs-source conflicts, surfaced for human review rather than silently overwritten.

🔁 Label Expansions — the 4,482-row extension (added 2026-09-16)

      
The single largest body of officially documented FDA efficacy decisions is not novel approvals — it is efficacy supplements: FDA decisions granting a new indication, new population, or new efficacy claim on an already-approved NDA/BLA. FDA approves roughly 130–600 of these per year versus ~50 novel drugs.

      
Why this was the right way to add 1,000+ new entries. A source link only claims a row matches its source. scripts/crosscheck_master_vs_openfda.py re-opens the primary openFDA Drugs@FDA record for all 1,427 novel-approval rows and compares sponsor, brand and decision date field by field, writing data/verification_crosscheck.csv. 🎯 Decision Engine — Scientific Literature & FDA Expertise

      
Site serves as decision engine ultimately to determine outcome of FDA decision and likelihood. Built using known scientific literature and FDA decision-making expertise:

      
    
        
  • Base rates: BIO / Biomedtracker / Informa, Clinical Development Success Rates 2011–2020 — NDA/BLA → approval 90.6% (n=1,453), Phase III → NDA/BLA 57.8%, Phase I → approval 7.9%, per-disease-area tables, per-modality tables
    • 
        
  • Accelerated approval: HHS OIG OEI-01-21-00401 (13% withdrawn), peer-reviewed accelerated-approval series (52% of 205 oncology indications converted to regular approval, 15% withdrawn, 33% ongoing; 77%/23% in 133-indication Lancet eClinicalMedicine series; 75% conversion in 57 non-oncology indications)
    • 
        
  • Review pathway: Priority Review = FDA judges drug potentially offers significant improvement — positive signal. Breakthrough Therapy = preliminary clinical evidence of substantial improvement — strong predictor. Accelerated Approval = surrogate endpoint, confirmatory trial risk — treated as risk factor (-0.5 weight)
    • 
        
  • Company track record: Repeat approvals = experienced regulatory/CMC organization. Prior CRL raises probability of review issues recurring. Repeat CRLs (Aldeyra/Reproxalap: 3 CRLs same indication) strong negative signal
    • 
        
  • Advisory committee: Favorable vote followed by approval in large majority; adverse vote FDA follows more often than not; convening committee signals contested benefit-risk
    •
