💊 DrugAnalysis

Publicly traded biotech & pharma companies tracked against FDA decisions, stock-price reactions, and clinical pipeline success rates — all sourced from official, verifiable links.

Core Analysis Table

Company, drug, FDA decision, stock-price reaction, and a pipeline success-rate summary — the single table for a fast overview. Empty price cells mean data has not yet been fetched/verified for that row (never estimated).

FDA Approvals Master List

100 verified FDA drug approvals (2024–2026), one row per decision, each with two source links for manual review.

Complete Response Letters (Rejections)

FDA rejections / regulatory setbacks for publicly traded companies — flagged as irregularities worth tracking, including repeat-CRL cases.

Stock Price Snapshots

Closing prices immediately before/after each FDA decision, sourced live from the Yahoo Finance chart API. Rows marked "Data unavailable" reflect delistings/acquisitions where historical data could not be retrieved — never a placeholder guess.

Company Pipeline Scorecards

Per-company breakdown of how many pipeline programs advanced, paused, or were rejected — a starter set focused on companies with the richest FDA-decision history in this dataset (more companies to be added over time).

About & Methodology

Sources

  • FDA decisions — FDA.gov Novel Drug Approvals pages, accessdata.fda.gov drug label PDFs, and the official openFDA CRL transparency API (api.fda.gov/transparency/crl.json).
  • Stock prices — Yahoo Finance chart API (query1.finance.yahoo.com), cross-referenced against stockanalysis.com where available.
  • Company/pipeline detail — company investor-relations press releases, SEC filings, and reputable biotech trade press (StockTitan, FierceBiotech, Endpoints-style coverage), always cited with a direct link.

Verification approach

Every row in the approvals and CRL tables carries a verification_status field. Rows are marked Verified only when a company/ticker/date combination was corroborated by at least one official or primary-source link. Rows with any ambiguity (e.g. disputed sponsor ticker, ambiguous stock-reaction causality, foreign-only listings) are explicitly flagged in the notes column rather than silently resolved.

Known limitations (in progress)

  • Stock-price snapshots are not yet complete for every decision in the master list — unmatched rows show blank price cells rather than estimates.
  • Company scorecards currently cover a focused subset of companies (CRL recipients + top FDA-decision-volume companies); broader coverage is planned.
  • Two tickers required manual correction after initial research (Xolremdi/X4 Pharmaceuticals → XFOR; Cardamyst/Milestone Pharmaceuticals → MIST) — both are now resolved and cited.

Raw data

All tables on this site are generated directly from CSV files in /data in the repository, so you can inspect, verify, or reuse the underlying data yourself.
